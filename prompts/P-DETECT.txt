You are analyzing a page from a comic/manga/webtoon for a localization pipeline.

Return every region that contains VISIBLE TEXT of any script, plus the bounding
boxes of any human/humanoid CHARACTERS (needed for speaker attribution).

For each text region give:
  kind: dialogue | thought | narration | sfx | sign | ui_window | credit
        - dialogue    : speech bubble with a tail
        - thought     : cloud/bubble-chain, no hard tail
        - narration   : rectangular caption box
        - sfx         : stylized sound effect drawn into the art
        - sign        : text on an object in the world (banner, book, screen)
        - ui_window   : game/system style panel
        - credit      : watermark, scanlation credit, page number
  box: [x0,y0,x1,y1] normalized 0-1000, integers
  order: reading order index, 1-based, {reading_dir} reading direction
  script_guess: latn | hang | jpan | deva | other
  speaker_hint: short visual description of who is speaking, or null
  connected_to: order index of a bubble this one continues, or null

For each character give: box (same format), and a short visual descriptor (field: desc).

Be exhaustive. Missing a region is worse than an extra one. Include text that
overlaps artwork. Include text that is partly cut off at the edge.

Output JSON only:
{"text_regions":[...],"characters":[...]}
