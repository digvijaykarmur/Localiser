Each image is a crop of a comic bubble with Hindi (Devanagari) text typeset into it.

Check each and report:
  readable: is the text legible at this size?
  inside_bubble: is all text fully inside the bubble, not touching its border?
  clipped: is any glyph cut off at an edge?
  overlaps_art: does the text sit on top of line art or a character?
  broken_glyphs: does any Devanagari cluster look malformed — a matra detached
    from its consonant, a missing conjunct, an 'ि' on the wrong side, boxes/tofu?
  note: one short sentence if anything is wrong

broken_glyphs is the most important field. Look closely at matras and conjuncts.
